Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This page explains what information delraycommercial.com collects when you visit or contact Damara Cohn, and how it's used. This site is operated by Damara Cohn, Broker-Owner of Mangrove Realty, Inc., serving commercial real estate clients from Lake Worth Beach to Deerfield Beach.

What This Site Collects

delraycommercial.com uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, general location, device type). It does not run advertising trackers or ad pixels, and analytics data is not used to build an advertising profile of you or sold to third parties. Standard technical information (IP address, browser type, pages requested) is also logged automatically by the hosting provider for security and performance.

When you voluntarily submit information through a form on this site — requesting a property valuation, submitting buyer criteria, starting a referral relationship, or asking a financing question — the site collects only what you enter, typically your name, phone number, email address, and property or transaction details relevant to your inquiry.

How Your Information Is Used

  • To respond to you directly — a valuation, a buyer match, a referral introduction, or a financing connection.
  • To connect you with a trusted lender or referral partner, only for financing or referral-partner inquiries you submit.
  • Information submitted through this site is not sold to third parties.

Text and Phone Contact

If you call or text the number listed on this site, that conversation is handled the same as any direct call or text to Damara Cohn or Mangrove Realty — it is not automated, and your number is not added to a marketing list without your consent.

Third-Party Links

This site links out to Mangrove Realty's property search tools, damaracohn.com, and other platforms. Once you leave delraycommercial.com, that platform's own privacy policy applies — this policy only covers delraycommercial.com itself.

Your Choices

You can ask to be removed from any list at any time by contacting Damara directly using the information below. You're never required to submit a form to browse this site or call/text directly.
