What Qualifies

Why Sell Direct

A buyer is already looking for what you own.

There's no centralized MLS for commercial property in this corridor, which means most owners never hear from a serious buyer until they've already signed with a national firm and started paying institutional fees. Private investors and 1031 exchange buyers dominate deals under $20M — they're actively looking, and they can't easily find inventory like yours either.

  • Direct relationship — you deal with me, not a transaction coordinator.
  • Local record — decades of Delray Beach to Boca Raton commercial and industrial sales.
  • Financing connections — I'll introduce you to a lender if a refinance makes more sense than a sale.
