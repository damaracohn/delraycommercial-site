Which commercial property types sell in Coconut Creek?

Coconut Creek is a growing Broward County city. Delray Commercial sells office, retail and mixed-use property there, plus multifamily, industrial, land and NNN assets.

Does Delray Commercial have a minimum price in Coconut Creek?

Coconut Creek sales are typically $1M and above, and Delray Commercial handles sales only, not leases.

Can Coconut Creek owners sell directly to the broker?

Yes. You work with Damara Cohn directly, with no committee, and an off-market sale is one option to discuss.

Why would a Coconut Creek seller consider a 1031 exchange?

A 1031 exchange may allow an investor to defer capital gains tax by reinvesting in qualifying property. The deadlines are strict, so involve a CPA or qualified intermediary first. Delray Commercial does not give tax advice.
