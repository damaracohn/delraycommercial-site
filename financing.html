How This Works

A direct introduction — nothing more.

I connect owners and buyers to trusted commercial lenders. I am not a mortgage lender or loan originator, and I do not process, underwrite, or negotiate loan terms — I make the introduction and step back.

Commercial and business-purpose loans are handled differently from residential mortgages under Florida law, and referral compensation on a pure introduction does not require a mortgage license.
