Delray Beach commercial real estate: common questions

What types of commercial property does Delray Commercial sell in Delray Beach?

Delray Commercial handles office, retail, mixed-use, multifamily, industrial, land and triple-net (NNN) property in Delray Beach, including buildings in and around the Atlantic Avenue corridor and nearby business districts.

Is there a minimum price for Delray Beach commercial sales?

Yes. Delray Commercial focuses on sales of $1M and up in Delray Beach. It handles sales only, so it does not lease or rent property.

Can I sell my Delray Beach commercial building without listing it publicly?

Often, yes. You deal directly with broker Damara Cohn rather than a committee, and a private, off-market sale can be discussed alongside a traditional listing.

How does a 1031 exchange affect selling an investment property in Delray Beach?

A 1031 exchange can let an investor defer capital gains tax by moving sale proceeds into another qualifying investment property. The rules and deadlines are strict, so involve your CPA or a qualified intermediary before you list. Delray Commercial is not a tax advisor.
