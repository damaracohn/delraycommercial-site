Common Questions

Lighthouse Point commercial real estate: common questions

What commercial property can be sold in Lighthouse Point?

Lighthouse Point is a smaller, affluent Broward County market with limited commercial inventory. Delray Commercial helps owners and buyers with the office, retail, mixed-use and other commercial property that does come up.

Does Delray Commercial handle small Lighthouse Point deals under $1M?

Delray Commercial focuses on Lighthouse Point sales of $1M and above. It does not handle leasing or rentals.

Is an off-market sale possible for a Lighthouse Point commercial property?

In a market with so little inventory, a private sale can make sense. Owners deal directly with broker Damara Cohn and can ask whether a quiet sale or a public listing fits better.

What should a Lighthouse Point investor know about 1031 exchanges?

A 1031 exchange can defer capital gains tax when sale proceeds go into another qualifying investment property. Replacement property can be hard to find in a tight market, so planning with a CPA or qualified intermediary before selling matters. Delray Commercial is not a tax advisor.
