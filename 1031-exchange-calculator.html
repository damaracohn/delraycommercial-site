How This Works

The two deadlines that actually matter

45 days to identify

From your closing date, you have 45 calendar days to formally identify replacement property in writing to your qualified intermediary. This is the deadline that trips people up — it runs out faster than it feels like it will.

180 days to close

You have 180 calendar days total from your closing date (not 180 days after identification) to close on the replacement property. Both clocks start on the same day and run at the same time.

Both are calendar days, not business days

Weekends and holidays count. There's no extension for a deadline that lands on a Sunday or Thanksgiving, except in rare, formally declared disaster-relief situations. Plan around the actual date this calculator gives you.

Start looking before you close

The most common way people blow the 45-day deadline is waiting until after closing to start looking at replacement options. If you know you're doing an exchange, start identifying candidates while your current sale is still under contract.
